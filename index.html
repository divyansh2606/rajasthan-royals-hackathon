How pressure is defined

Pressure is a match state, not a reputation.

01

Late innings

Every ball from the 16th over onwards enters the primary pressure scenario.

02

Chase stress

Chases qualify earlier when the required rate reaches 9 or higher with ten overs or fewer remaining.

03

Wickets in hand

Chase balls with five or more wickets down receive pressure status during the last ten overs.

04

Knockout games

Final-eight-over deliveries in knockout matches are included to capture tournament stakes.
